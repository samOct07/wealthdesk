# BNB Gold Loan Guide

## What Is a Gold Loan

A BNB Gold Loan is secured by eligible gold that you pledge to the bank. BNB assesses the gold before confirming the amount it can offer. The pledged gold is held by the bank until the loan is fully repaid and closed.

## Loan Amount and Gold Valuation

The eligible loan amount is based on BNB's assessment of the gold and the applicable loan-to-value limit. BNB may lend up to 75% of the assessed gold value. The final amount is subject to the bank's valuation and approval; the purchase price of an item does not determine its loan value.

Ask the branch to explain the valuation and the amount offered before accepting the loan. Keep the valuation details and pledge receipt with your loan documents.

## Required Documents

Document requirements can depend on the application and applicable rules. Before visiting, confirm the current checklist with a BNB branch. Bring:

- Government-issued proof of identity and address
- PAN card, if required for your loan
- Bank account details for disbursement and repayment
- The gold items you want BNB to assess
- Any available purchase or ownership documents for the gold

BNB may ask for additional documents during verification.

## Application Process

1. Apply at a BNB branch and submit the requested documents.
2. Present the gold for assessment. BNB will explain the valuation and the loan amount available.
3. Review the loan agreement carefully, including the interest rate, repayment schedule, charges, and the terms that apply if payments are missed.
4. If you accept the offer and the application is approved, BNB completes the loan formalities and disburses the funds through its approved method.
5. Keep the loan agreement, valuation details, and pledge receipt for your records.

## Repayment and Release of Gold

Make payments according to the schedule and method in your loan agreement. Contact your branch if you need help understanding a due date or repayment option.

After all outstanding amounts have been paid, request loan closure and follow BNB's verification process to collect the pledged gold. Bring your pledge receipt and identification, and check the returned items against the receipt before leaving the branch.

## If You Miss a Payment

Missed payments may result in additional charges or other actions described in your loan agreement. If you expect difficulty making a payment, contact BNB promptly to discuss your account. Review the agreement for the notices and recovery process that may apply if the loan remains unpaid.
