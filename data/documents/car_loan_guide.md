# BNB Car Loan Guide

## Who is Eligible

A BNB Car Loan is available to salaried individuals, self-employed professionals, and business owners who meet the following criteria:

- Indian resident, 21 to 65 years of age
- Minimum 1 year of continuous employment (salaried) or 2 years of business continuity (self-employed)
- Credit score of 680 or above (CIBIL or equivalent bureau score)
- Minimum net monthly income of Rs. 20,000

Joint applications are permitted with a spouse or immediate family member as co-applicant.

## Loan Amount and LTV

BNB finances up to 90% of the on-road price of the vehicle for new cars. For used cars (up to 5 years old), the maximum LTV is 70% of the valuation or invoice price, whichever is lower.

The minimum loan amount is Rs. 1 lakh. There is no fixed upper cap — the amount is determined by your income eligibility and the vehicle price.

## Interest Rate and Tenure

Car loans at BNB start from 9.5% per annum on a reducing balance basis. The rate depends on your credit score, loan tenure, and vehicle type (new or used).

Loan tenure ranges from 1 year to 7 years. Shorter tenure means higher EMI but less total interest. Use the BNB EMI calculator at bnb.co.in to compare options.

## Required Documents

**Salaried applicants:**
- PAN card and Aadhaar card
- Last 3 months' salary slips
- Last 6 months' bank statement (salary account)
- Latest Form 16

**Self-employed applicants:**
- PAN card and Aadhaar card
- Last 2 years' ITR with computation
- Last 6 months' business bank statement

**Vehicle documents (all applicants):**
- Proforma invoice from the dealer (for new cars)
- RC book and insurance copy (for used cars)
- Valuation certificate from a BNB-approved valuer (for used cars)

## Application Process

1. Apply online at bnb.co.in/loans/car-loan or visit any BNB branch.
2. Submit the required documents. A Relationship Manager will contact you within 1 working day.
3. Loan sanction within 3 to 5 working days of document submission.
4. Disbursement directly to the dealer's account for new cars. For used cars, disbursement is to the seller's account after RC verification.

## Processing Fee

A processing fee applies at the time of application. Ask your Relationship Manager or branch for the current fee applicable to your loan amount. Processing fees are non-refundable once the credit appraisal begins.

## Prepayment

Partial prepayment is allowed after 6 months from the first EMI. No prepayment charges apply after 12 months from disbursement. Before 12 months, a prepayment charge of 2% of the outstanding principal applies.

## Insurance

Comprehensive motor insurance is mandatory for the loan tenure. BNB offers insurance tie-ups at competitive rates — ask your Relationship Manager for details. The insurance must name BNB as the hypothecatee until the loan is fully repaid.
